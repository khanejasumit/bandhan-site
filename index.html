Bandhan

Keep in touch with the people who matter.

An Android app that tells you who is worth a message today, and why, and writes it for you.

What Bandhan does

  • Reads the contacts on your phone and, if you sign in with Google, your Google Contacts, to know birthdays, anniversaries and workplaces.
  • Each day shows who is worth a message and why: a birthday or anniversary, a festival, a new job or work anniversary, someone you have not spoken to in a while, a follow-up you promised, a meeting you had.
  • Writes the message for you, ready-made in four tones or with AI when you ask, and opens WhatsApp, Gmail, Outlook or LinkedIn with it written. You press send.
  • Optional connections you can turn on: Gmail (LinkedIn's notification emails, for job changes), Google Calendar and Tasks, Calendly, Plaud, Fireflies and Notion.

Your contact list stays on your phone. Bandhan sends only two things itself, both your choice: an email you tap "Send from Gmail" on, and a Gmail wish you scheduled, at 8 am on the day.

Where it is

Bandhan is in private testing on Android. It is not on Google Play yet.
