Delete your Bandhan account

In the app

Open You › Privacy & Data › Delete my account. This deletes your account, its cloud backup, and all Bandhan data on that phone, immediately.

Without the app

Email khanejas@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Bandhan account". We delete the account and its backup within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account and your cloud backup (groups, tags, notes, follow-ups, schedules, message history). Nothing is kept.
