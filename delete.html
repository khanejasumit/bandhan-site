Bandhan

Delete your Bandhan account

In the app

Open You › Privacy & Data › Delete my account. This deletes your account, every copy of its cloud backup, and all Bandhan data on that phone, immediately. If the app tells you some of the backup’s files could not be removed just then and you choose “Delete anyway”, your account still goes at once; write to us as below and we will remove the files that were left.

Without the app

Email khanejas@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Bandhan account". We delete the account and its backup within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account and every copy of your cloud backup — your groups, tags, notes, follow-ups, schedules and message history; scheduled wishes; your own wishes, cards and settings; and your Ideas set-up, drafts, posts, saved stories and post numbers — and, if you connected LinkedIn in Ideas, that connection. Nothing is kept, with the one exception above: backup files the server could not remove at that moment, which stay unreadable by any account until we remove them.
